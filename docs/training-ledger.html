In-game

Standing rules, not habits to remember

Both tied to something you already see during a game — not a move count, not a mental checklist.

Signal: clock shows under 4:00 Slow down on your next move, especially a capture or a check. Your blunder rate roughly doubles below 4 minutes remaining (13% → 24% under 30s). You already think longer on moves you get wrong than clean ones — this isn't "play faster" or "play slower" in general, it's specifically that under-4:00 moment being the flag.
Signal: you just lost Spend 2 minutes on it before queuing the next game. Chess.com's own Game Review, or just scroll back through your own moves. The habit is "before the next game," not "sometime later" — later doesn't happen.
